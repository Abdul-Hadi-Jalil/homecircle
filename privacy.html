Privacy Policy

HomeCircle: one home, every detail connected.

Effective date: 27 November 2026

HomeCircle ("the app", "we", "us") is a household organiser for Android. It lets family members share an inventory, tasks and repair requests, a shopping list and a household chat. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and the display name you enter when you register.
  • Your password, which is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and stored only in hashed form. We never see it in plain text.

Household content you create

  • Household names, descriptions and the avatar you choose.
  • Household membership and roles (admin or member) and invitation codes.
  • Inventory items, categories, quantities, locations and notes.
  • Photos you choose to attach to inventory items or tasks. The app only accesses the specific photo you pick. It does not scan your gallery.
  • Tasks, repair requests, comments, shopping items and chat messages.
  • An activity history of changes made in the household, so members can see who did what.

Device and technical information

  • A push notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, used only to deliver household notifications to your device.
  • Your notification preferences.
  • Standard server logs (such as IP address, request time and error details) kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting.

HomeCircle does not collect your location, contacts, call logs or advertising identifiers. It contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show household content to the members of that household, and only to them.
  • To send notifications you have enabled, such as task assignments, low-stock alerts and chat messages.
  • To keep the service reliable and secure, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Who can see your information

Content you add to a household is visible to the other members of that household. Access is enforced on the server with row-level security. Your email address is used for your account and is not shown to people outside your households.

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and photo storage.
  • Render: hosting for the HomeCircle API.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notification delivery.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage. Server access to household data is checked for every request. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and household content while your account is active. You can delete individual items, leave a household, or, as a household admin, delete a household inside the app.

You can delete your account at any time. Follow the steps on the account deletion page, which you can also reach from Profile → Delete account in the app. We delete your account and personal data within 30 days of a verified request. Content you created inside a household that other members still use is deleted or anonymised. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Children

HomeCircle is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your display name and change your password in the app.
  • Turn notifications on or off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
