HomeCircle by Abdul Hadi Jalil

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to abdulhadijalil34@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my HomeCircle account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to HomeCircle, so we can verify the account is yours.
  3. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

You can also open this page from the app: Profile → Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name and sign-in credentials.
  • Your household memberships and roles.
  • Your device push notification tokens and notification preferences.
  • Households where you are the only member, with all of their inventory, photos, tasks, shopping items and chat messages.

What may be kept

  • Content you created inside a household that other members still use, such as inventory items, tasks or chat messages, is deleted or anonymised so it is no longer linked to you.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security, then deleted.
  • Information we must keep to meet a legal obligation, only for as long as required.

Delete some data without deleting your account

Inside the app you can delete inventory items, tasks and shopping items, leave a household, or, as an admin, delete a household. To have other specific data deleted, email us at the address above.
